Last Signal Stories connection: data-use notice

This personal automation connects the operator’s own Last Signal Stories YouTube channel. It is not a public service offering channel access to other people.

It reads the channel’s video status and public video statistics, and published comments and replies. Comment IDs and author channel IDs are used to avoid counting the same viewer more than once. It does not read private messages, send replies, moderate comments, or delete YouTube videos.

With the operator’s Google consent, the workflow uploads videos privately, sets their metadata and cover, and schedules approved videos for future publication. Access may be revoked at any time through Google Account → Third-party connections. Revoking access stops new authenticated work; videos already uploaded remain in YouTube until managed there.

Google connection credentials and the image-provider token are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used by GitHub-hosted jobs. They are not committed to the public repository. Working media is stored in a private draft GitHub release. Production reports and audience evidence are stored in the private automation repository or authenticated workflow artifacts. This public information site does not display viewer comments, account identifiers, tokens or working media. Artifacts have configured retention periods; repository history and draft media persist until the operator removes them.

Fictional scene descriptions and reference images are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI for image generation and quality review. Dialogue text is sent to Microsoft’s voice service. No payment details are used by this workflow.

For questions or removal requests concerning project records, contact the operator through the public project at https://github.com/paul984021-hash/aieverything98-automation . No additional data collection, advertising, or sale of viewer data is implemented by the automation.
